**Description**:

Kalyx Wallet is a non-custodial crypto wallet: only you hold your keys.

YOUR KEYS STAY WITH YOU
• Your recovery phrase and keys are encrypted on this computer with your password (scrypt + AES-256-GCM).
• Kalyx has no account server: it can't see, freeze or recover your funds.
• Automatic lock after a delay you choose.

ETHEREUM, EVM NETWORKS AND SOLANA
• One address for Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, BNB Chain and the other EVM networks; one Solana address.
• Balances and activity across every network, valued in your currency.
• Create a wallet, or import a 12- to 24-word phrase, a private key or a backup from the Kalyx mobile app.

SEND WITHOUT MISTAKES
• Look-alike address alert: Kalyx spots an address that resembles one you've paid before.
• ENS names, slow / normal / fast or manual fees (capped).
• Whitelist: when on, Kalyx only sends to addresses on your list. Adding an address or turning protection off only takes effect after 24 h.

DAPPS, SAFELY
• Works with EIP-1193 and EIP-6963 (EVM) and Wallet Standard (Solana).
• Every request opens in a separate Kalyx window that the site can't cover or imitate.
• Before you sign, Kalyx explains what you'll lose and receive, flags unlimited approvals and risky contracts, and lets you reduce an approval.
• Manage and disconnect connected sites in one click.

Kalyx will never ask for your recovery phrase. Nobody should have it.
